Arcade — Vector Glow
Multi-pass Kawase blur bloom for Asteroids/Vectrex style neon phosphor vectors.
Scaler — ScaleFX AA Fast
Subpixel gradient edge-directed anti-aliasing without blurry bilinear filtering.
